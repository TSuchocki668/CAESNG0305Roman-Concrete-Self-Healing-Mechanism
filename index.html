How Lime Clasts Could Seal a Crack

1. Hot mixing leaves lime clasts
During mixing, quicklime reacted with water and released heat. Lime-rich fragments, called lime clasts, remained in the hardened concrete.
2. A crack runs through a clast
In the ancient samples researchers studied, cracks ran mainly through lime clasts. Water entering a crack reaches the exposed lime.
3. Calcium dissolves
Water dissolves calcium from the clast, forming a calcium-rich solution that spreads along the crack.
4. Calcium carbonate fills the crack
Calcium carbonate crystallizes from the solution inside the crack. In laboratory tests, the new mineral filled the crack and stopped water flowing through.
